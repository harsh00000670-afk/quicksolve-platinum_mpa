What is FOIR?

Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio is the maximum % of your salary a bank allows for ALL your EMIs combined.

If you earn ₹50k and FOIR is 50%, banks will only allow your total EMIs (new + old) to reach ₹25k. Higher income gets a higher FOIR limit!
