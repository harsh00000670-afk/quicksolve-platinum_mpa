PRIVACY CORE
STATUS: Your Data Stays on Your Device.
1. Zero Server Architecture
QuickSolve Platinum operates on a Client-Side Architecture. We do not have a backend database. All calculations (Loan, Currency, Programmer) happen locally on your device's processor. No data leaves your phone to reach our servers.
2. Use of Local Storage
We use your browser's LocalStorage database to save preferences, currency cache, and history. This data is strictly sandboxed to your browser and cannot be accessed by other apps. Clearing your browser cache will reset the app.
3. Financial Calculations
Data entered in Loan/EMI/GST calculators is processed using standard mathematical formulas. This data is volatile (deleted when you close the tab) unless explicitly saved by you. We do not track your loan amounts or income.
4. Third-Party Services (Currency)
For live currency rates, we fetch data from ExchangeRate-API. Your IP address may be visible to these providers during the request, which is standard internet protocol. We do not send your personal amounts to them, only the base currency code.
5. Sensitive Code/Binary Data
Inputs in the Programmer Calculator (Hex, Bin, ASCII) are processed via BigInt JavaScript Engine locally. We do not log, store, or transmit your code snippets or binary data.
6. Clipboard Permissions
The app may request access to your clipboard ONLY when you explicitly click a "Copy" or "Paste" button. We do not background-read your clipboard history.
7. Service Workers & Caching
This app installs a Service Worker to function offline. It caches the app's core files (HTML/CSS/JS) on your device. This allows the app to load instantly without internet.
