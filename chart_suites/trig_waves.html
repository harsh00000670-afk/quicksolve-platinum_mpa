WAVE MECHANICS & DOMAINS

The Standard Form:
y = A * func(B(x - C)) + D
A (Amplitude): How tall the wave gets from its center line. Negative 'A' flips the wave upside down.
B (Frequency): How fast the wave oscillates. For inverses, it stretches or shrinks the valid X domain.
C (Phase Shift): Moves the entire wave left or right along the X-axis.
D (Vertical Shift): Moves the center line of the wave up or down the Y-axis.
*Functions like Tangent, Secant, and Cosecant shoot to infinity (Asymptotes). Inverse functions (ArcSin, ArcCos) only exist in specific constrained domains!
