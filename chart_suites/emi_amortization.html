FRONT-LOADED INTEREST

Why Does the Graph Look Like That?
Look at the red bars in the early years. The bank structures your EMI so that you pay off almost all of their interest first, before your actual loan amount (principal) starts decreasing significantly.
The Math Trick

1. Every month, interest is calculated on your Remaining Balance.

2. Because the balance is highest at the beginning, the interest portion of your EMI is massive.

3. If you prepay extra principal in the first few years, you bypass this trap and save lakhs!
