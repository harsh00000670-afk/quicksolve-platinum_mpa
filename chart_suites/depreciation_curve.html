DEPRECIATION METHODS

Why Does it Matter?
Different assets lose value differently. A laptop loses half its value in year 1, while furniture loses value steadily over 10 years.
1. Straight-Line (SL)

The asset loses the exact same amount of value every year until it hits its scrap value. Best for steady-use items like furniture.

2. Double Declining Balance (DDB)

An accelerated method. The asset loses massive value in the first few years. Best for tech, computers, and vehicles which become outdated quickly.

3. Sum-of-the-Years'-Digits (SYD)

A smoother accelerated method. It depreciates heavily early on, but not as aggressively as DDB. Often used for heavy machinery.
