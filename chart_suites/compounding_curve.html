THE 8TH WONDER

Linear vs Exponential:
Notice how the Blue Line (Your Money) goes up in a straight line, but the Green Curve (Total Wealth) shoots upward like a rocket!
The Snowball Effect

Year 1: You earn interest on your initial money.

Year 2: You earn interest on your money AND the interest from Year 1.

Year 10+: The interest starts earning so much interest itself, that it overtakes your actual deposits!
