The Intersection (Target Acquired):
The exact point where the Green Line (Revenue) crosses above the Red Line (Total Costs) is your Break-Even Point.
The 3 Zones

1. The Danger Zone (Left): Before the intersection, your costs are higher than your sales. You are losing money.

2. Ground Zero (Intersection): You have exactly paid off your fixed costs. Profit is exactly ₹0.

3. The Profit Zone (Right): After the intersection, every single unit sold adds directly to your pure profit!
